Which braid styles are generally quicker, and which take longer?

The points below come from general hair-styling sources, not from Spa Bali Moon's own timings. Use them as a rough guide only.

  • Dutch braids are among the quicker styles. One hair guide says Dutch braids take only a few minutes to complete [3].
  • Cornrows are smaller braids worked close to the scalp, and the same guide notes they can sometimes take several hours [3].
  • Fishtail braids look delicate, but their main challenge is the time they take to do [4].
  • Box braids on a full head are a long job. One salon booking platform puts installation at about four to eight hours, depending on braid size, the braider's skill and hair texture [2].

Because our sessions run 30 minutes to 2 hours, please ask on WhatsApp how many braids your chosen box braid or cornrow style includes, and how long it will take on your hair.

Will braids damage my hair?

Braids are a gentle style when they are not too tight. The American Academy of Dermatology (AAD) warns that hairstyles which pull on the hair can lead to traction alopecia, a type of hair loss that can become permanent the longer it goes untreated [1].

The AAD lists signs that a style is too tight: pain from tightly pulled hair, stinging on the scalp, crusts on the scalp, and "tenting", where sections of the scalp are pulled up [1]. It also suggests loosening braids near the hairline, choosing thicker braids, and removing extensions straight away if they hurt [1].

At Spa Bali Moon we section and prepare your hair first and avoid excessive tension. If any braid feels painful, tell your therapist at once so it can be loosened.
