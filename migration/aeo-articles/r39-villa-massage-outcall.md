# Can You Get a Massage in Your Villa in Bali? How Outcall Massage Works

Yes. In Bali you can book a massage in your villa, hotel room or home: a therapist travels to you with a massage bed, fresh linen and oils, and treats you in your room or on your terrace. With [Spa Bali Moon's outcall massage](/outcall-home-service-massage/), treatments cost the same as in our Seminyak spa plus IDR 75.000 per therapist, so a one-hour Balinese massage is IDR 234.000 at your villa, and in Seminyak and nearby areas a therapist usually arrives within 30 to 60 minutes.

## What is an outcall massage?

An outcall massage is a professional massage given wherever you are staying, instead of at the spa. In Bali it is also called home service, mobile massage or in-villa massage. The therapist brings the equipment, sets up in your villa, hotel room or home, and gives the same treatment you would have in the spa.

It is a convenient choice after a long flight or a full day exploring Bali, or simply when you would rather relax in the privacy of your own space. Solo travellers, couples, families and groups can all book it.

## How does an in-villa massage work, step by step?

Booking an in-villa massage with Spa Bali Moon takes a few messages on WhatsApp. You send your location and preferred time, we confirm the therapist and the total price, and the therapist arrives with everything needed. Setup takes about five to ten minutes, and your treatment time starts once the bed is ready.

1. **Message us.** Send your villa or hotel name, room number, the treatment and length, the number of guests and your preferred time to +62 878-6317-5144.
2. **Get confirmation.** We confirm availability and the total price, including IDR 75.000 per therapist, before the therapist sets off.
3. **The therapist arrives.** In Seminyak, Petitenget, Kerobokan, Canggu and North Kuta, this is usually within 30 to 60 minutes, depending on traffic.
4. **Setup.** The therapist sets up the massage bed in about five to ten minutes. A booked 60-minute massage is a full 60 minutes of treatment.
5. **Your treatment.** Tell the therapist the pressure you like and any areas that need attention. You can ask for lighter or firmer pressure at any time.
6. **Pack down.** Packing away takes a similar time, and the therapist takes everything with them.

## What does the therapist bring, and what do you need to prepare?

A Spa Bali Moon therapist brings a portable massage bed, freshly laundered linen and towels, professional massage oils and any equipment your treatment needs, and nothing is reused between guests. You only need a clear space of roughly two by two metres: a bedroom, a terrace or a living area all work well.

Not every mobile provider in Bali brings a table. A 2026 Canggu guide notes that some therapists expect to work on your bed or on a mat, so it is worth asking when you book [2]. For a massage outdoors, another guide suggests a flat, shaded spot [1]. If you have a favourite place, such as the terrace by the pool, tell us when you book.

## How much does an in-villa massage cost in Bali?

In 2026, mobile therapists in Bali typically charge about IDR 150.000 to IDR 350.000 per person for a one-hour massage, and some add a callout or travel fee [1][2]. At Spa Bali Moon, home service costs the same as our spa price plus IDR 75.000 per therapist, so one hour of Balinese massage is IDR 234.000.

A hotel's own spa usually costs more. Five-star resort menus in Bali list a one-hour Balinese massage at about IDR 800.000 to IDR 1.800.000, and many add a 10% service charge and 11% government tax at checkout [3]. Prices vary by area and season, so always ask for the total.

## Do hotels in Bali allow outside massage therapists?

Some do and some do not. Larger hotels and resorts with their own spa are the most likely to restrict outside therapists, while private villas and guesthouses rarely do. Check with reception before you book. If your hotel does not allow home service, you are welcome at our Seminyak spa on Jl. Pangkung Sari instead.

## Is an in-villa massage safe and hygienic?

Every Spa Bali Moon home visit is prepared with clean linen, sanitised equipment and quality massage oils, and nothing is reused between guests. Massage appears to carry few risks when given by a trained practitioner, but tell your therapist about any health conditions so they can take precautions, and speak up if anything hurts [5].

If you have a medical condition, an injury or have recently had surgery, check with your doctor before booking [5]. Our treatments are professional massage and spa therapy, and our terms include a zero-tolerance policy on inappropriate behaviour towards therapists.
