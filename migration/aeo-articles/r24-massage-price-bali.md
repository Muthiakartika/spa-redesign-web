# How Much Is a Massage in Bali? Seminyak Price Guide

A one-hour massage in Bali usually costs about IDR 100.000 to IDR 600.000 at a day spa, IDR 150.000 to IDR 350.000 when a therapist comes to your villa, and IDR 450.000 to more than IDR 1.800.000 at a hotel or resort spa, depending on the area and the venue [1][2][3][4][5]. At [Spa Bali Moon in Seminyak](/seminyak/), a one-hour Balinese massage is IDR 159.000 per person, 30-minute massages start at IDR 90.000, and home service at your villa or hotel adds IDR 75.000 per therapist.

## How much does a massage cost in Bali in 2026?

Prices vary widely: recent 2026 guides put a one-hour massage at about IDR 100.000 to IDR 250.000 in a local massage shop, IDR 250.000 to IDR 600.000 in a mid-range day spa, and IDR 800.000 to IDR 1.800.000 in a five-star resort spa [2][3]. Most of the difference comes from the setting and facilities rather than the massage itself [3].

These are ranges, not fixed prices. Rates change with the season and the neighbourhood, and specialist treatments such as hot stone or deep tissue usually cost more than a standard Balinese massage [4]. Always check the current menu before you book.

A day spa is usually the best value for a quality massage, an in-villa therapist saves you the journey for a similar price, and a hotel or resort spa costs the most because you also pay for the facilities. ### What does a day spa massage cost in Bali?

A day spa is a standalone spa you visit for treatments, without a hotel attached. In 2026 most mid-range day spas in Bali charge about IDR 250.000 to IDR 600.000 for an hour [2][3], and two- to three-hour packages cost about IDR 350.000 to IDR 900.000 [2].

Spa Bali Moon's prices start below that range. A one-hour [Balinese massage](/seminyak/balinese-massage/) is IDR 159.000, and a one-hour [deep tissue massage](/seminyak/deep-tissue-massage/) is IDR 269.000, with towels, massage oil and a private treatment room included.

### How much does an in-villa massage cost in Bali?

Mobile therapists who come to your villa typically charge about IDR 150.000 to IDR 300.000 per person for a one-hour Balinese massage [4]. A 2026 Canggu guide puts sixty minutes at IDR 250.000 to IDR 350.000 and ninety minutes at IDR 350.000 to IDR 550.000 [5]. Some providers add a callout fee, so ask for the total before you confirm [4].

At Spa Bali Moon, outcall treatments cost the same as in the spa, plus IDR 75.000 per therapist. One hour of Balinese massage at your villa is therefore IDR 234.000. See [outcall and home service massage](/outcall-home-service-massage/) for the areas we cover and arrival times.

### Why do hotel spa massages cost more?

Hotel and resort spas charge for the setting as well as the treatment: lounges, robes, pools and views [3]. In Seminyak, a 60-minute hotel spa massage costs roughly IDR 450.000 to IDR 900.000 [1], and five-star resort menus across Bali list a one-hour Balinese massage at about IDR 800.000 to IDR 1.800.000 [3].

One 2026 guide estimates that standalone spas charge one-third to half of resort rates for comparable hands-on work [3]. If you are staying at a hotel, you can also book a [villa or hotel massage](/villa-hotel-massage/) from a day spa nearby, as long as your hotel allows outside therapists.

## Do Bali spas add tax, service charge or tips?

Many hotel and resort spas in Bali quote prices "++", which means tax and service are added at checkout. Many five-star menus add a 10% service charge and 11% government tax, about 21% in total [3], and another 2026 guide puts the addition at 15 to 21% for resort spas [2]. Ask whether a price is nett before you book.

Spa Bali Moon's website prices are the rates you pay in the spa, and for home service the only addition is IDR 75.000 per therapist.

### How much should you tip for a massage in Bali?

Tipping is optional in Bali. Recent guides suggest about IDR 20.000 to IDR 50.000 for a standard hour at a local or mid-range spa, and checking resort bills first, where service may already be included [2][4][5]. At Spa Bali Moon, tipping is not required and never added to your bill; a tip is appreciated but entirely up to you.

## How can you get the best value from a massage in Bali?

- **Compare like with like.** Check the price for the same length of treatment, because menus mix 60, 70 and 90-minute sessions [1].
- **Look at packages.** At Spa Bali Moon, spa packages start at IDR 299.000 per person, and a one-hour Balinese massage with a 30-minute manicure and a 30-minute pedicure is IDR 399.000.
- **Check what is added.** Hotel spas may add tax and service [3]; mobile therapists may add a callout or travel fee [4].
- **Book ahead in busy months.** Mobile providers may need three to five days' notice for popular slots in July, August and December [4]. Walk-ins are welcome at Spa Bali Moon, but booking on WhatsApp is recommended at busy times.
- **Choose the length you need.** If you only want your back or feet worked on, a 30-minute session costs less than a full-body hour.
