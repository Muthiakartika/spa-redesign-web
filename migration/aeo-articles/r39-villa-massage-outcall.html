What is an outcall massage?

An outcall massage is a professional massage given wherever you are staying, instead of at the spa. In Bali it is also called home service, mobile massage or in-villa massage. The therapist brings the equipment, sets up in your villa, hotel room or home, and gives the same treatment you would have in the spa.

It is a convenient choice after a long flight or a full day exploring Bali, or simply when you would rather relax in the privacy of your own space. Solo travellers, couples, families and groups can all book it.
